# Smart Sales Meeting Intelligence (SSMI)

## Overview

Smart Sales Meeting Intelligence (SSMI) is an AI-powered application designed specifically for sales professionals to capture the essence of customer meetings without requiring manual note-taking or reviewing entire recordings.

The application intelligently records a meeting, identifies important discussion segments, generates concise meeting notes, and creates a timeline of critical business conversations. Instead of replaying an hour-long recording, users can directly jump to the most valuable parts of the meeting.

The system supports both **live meetings** and **uploaded audio recordings**.

---

# Problem Statement

Sales professionals attend multiple customer meetings every day. Remembering customer requirements, pricing discussions, commitments, objections, and follow-up actions across many meetings is difficult.

Traditional recording applications save the complete conversation, forcing users to replay long recordings.

This project solves that problem by automatically identifying, bookmarking, and summarizing only the important moments.

# Core Features

## 1. One-Touch Meeting Start

The salesperson taps **Start Meeting**.

The application:
- Creates a meeting session
- Starts recording
- Begins AI analysis

---

## 2. Intelligent Recording

Instead of treating every second equally, AI continuously analyzes:

- Customer requirements
- Pricing
- Budget
- Negotiation
- Objections
- Decisions
- Action items
- Purchase intent

Only meaningful discussion segments are highlighted.

---

## 3. Voice Gesture Bookmarking

The salesperson can make a predefined voice cue (for example, a whistle, tongue click, or custom spoken keyword).

When detected:

- Current timestamp is bookmarked.
- AI prioritizes the surrounding discussion.
- The important segment is added to the meeting timeline.

No manual interaction is required.

---

## 4. Voice Gesture to Stop Meeting

Another predefined voice cue ends the meeting recording hands-free.

---

## 5. AI Timeline Generation

Example:

| From | To | Topic |
|------|----|-------|
| 02:14 | 03:01 | Customer Requirements |
| 08:30 | 09:42 | Budget Discussion |
| 15:20 | 16:10 | Pricing |
| 20:15 | 21:05 | Final Decision |

---

## 6. AI Meeting Summary

After processing, the system generates:

- Meeting objective
- Key discussion points
- Decisions
- Risks
- Follow-up actions
- Customer sentiment

---

## 7. Action Item Extraction

Automatically identifies tasks such as:

- Send quotation
- Schedule demo
- Share proposal
- Arrange technical meeting
- Follow-up date

---

## 8. Uploaded Audio Analysis

Supported formats include:

- MP3
- WAV
- M4A
- AAC

The same AI pipeline generates:

- Transcription
- Timeline
- Summary
- Action items

---

# User Workflow

1. Open the application.
2. Tap **Start Meeting**.
3. Conduct the meeting.
4. Use a predefined voice cue to bookmark important discussions.
5. Use another voice cue to stop recording.
6. AI processes the recording.
7. Review summaries, timelines, and action items.
